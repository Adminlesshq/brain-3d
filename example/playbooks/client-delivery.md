# Client delivery playbook

Every project moves through four stages: discovery, design, build, handover. Nothing moves to the next stage without written sign-off from the client.

## Stages
1. Discovery call, notes saved under `meetings/`
2. Design round with two revisions included
3. Build and testing
4. Handover with a short training call

Pricing rules live in [[Sales process]]. Big changes in how we deliver get written up in `decisions/log.md`.
